# Art & identity decisions log (Matt's reactions; keep updated every round)

Matt is happy to iterate on style and colour as long as needed until it is right. Every new round must respect everything below and say what it changes.

## Decided
- Identity concept: **The Hollow** (grey drained world; spirit light restores colour and leaves luminous trails; enemies spread corruption; one life-map system). See docs/IDENTITY.md.
- Art family: **polished chunky 2D cartoon, mood P1 WARM LANTERN** (LOCKED 2026-10-14, after the 3D proof: real 3D was richer but generic and too heavy for phones, needs custom models we cannot hire; 3D parked, branch spike-3d kept).
- Every character and enemy has its own unique, recognisable design. Hero, boss and signature spirits are custom for Hollowlight.
- Everything must fit the look and make mechanical sense (CLAUDE.md standing rule).
- Name: Hollowlight: Spirits of the Dusk. Chapter 1: Cursed Forest.
- Keep from earlier work: modern interface direction is wanted, but the specific UI look is NOT decided.

- Mood preference (2026-10-14): P1 WARM LANTERN (cosy, golden, cream-amber spirits on a cool grey-blue Hollow) over P2 Moonwell. Polished 2D cartoon mock-ups were well received (Hollow vs restored colour split, appealing creatures, node-web skill tree). Cream-amber ghost wolves (differs from bible moon-blue): assumed OK unless Matt says otherwise. FINAL art family (polished 2D cartoon vs 3D) still waits for the 3D proof comparison.

- Within 2D cartoon the DETAILS stay open (Matt wants to explore more ideas and find what is best): line style, shading, proportions, world rendering, colour, UI. A style-lab round compares variants on one shared scene (docs/mockups/style-lab/).

- STYLE RECIPE CHOSEN: HOLLOWLIGHT STYLE v1 (Matt, 2026-10-14) = mix of S1 Storybook Glow + S5 Heroic Cartoon: LINE L2 (tinted outline, a darker shade of each fill) + SHADE H2 (soft gradient with rim light) + PROPORTIONS P1 (chibi: big head, small and round, cosy and appealing) + WORLD W2 (richly textured painted ground with grass/flower detail) + LIGHT G1 lantern pool plus long glowing trails behind lantern, scarf and spirits (from G2). Mood P1 Warm Lantern. See docs/mockups/style-lab/.

- Creative freedom: designs do NOT have to follow docs/BIBLE.md; Matt is happy for any ideas that fit the game (identity, mechanics, style). The bible is a starting point.

- WREN DIRECTION (2026-10-14): Take C "Pelt Walker" (alpha wolf-pelt hood, shadowed face with amber glints, amber paw stitches, lantern ember floating on a thread) PLUS a wolf pup companion. v1 (round teal hood), A Lantern Keeper and B Pack Pup are not chosen. Next: Wren v2 sheet. Open idea for the abilities workshop: the pup could visually tie to Ghost Wolves ranks (visual only unless approved).

## Rejected (and why)
- Round 1 UI directions D1-D4 (glass, gothic, neon, ember): better than the original but "still blocky", "too much at once", "no identity of its own".
- Round 2 identity directions E1-E3 (ink, comic cel, papercut): disliked ("I don't like either"); picked "stylised 3D-looking", "too plain/simple", "characters not appealing", "wrong mood for the game".
- Pixel art and the flat vector look: too blocky/plain.
- Original live look (v0.2.0): "boring plain old game".

## Liked
- Wants "Borderlands 4 UI"-like boldness and a clear identity (restraint, one strong idea).
- Liked all three Hollow identity ideas (colour returns, spirit-painted trails, creeping corruption).
- Round 1 mock-ups were "better directions" than the live UI.

- First reference game: Spirit Hunters: Infinite Horde. Matt loves how it LOOKED (hence stylised 3D-looking) and its SKILL TREE: large pannable web, nodes lighting up, weapons/abilities unlocking from the tree, meaningful branch choices. Learn from it; do not copy art, names or layouts (research: docs/research/19-spirit-hunters-reference.md when ready).
