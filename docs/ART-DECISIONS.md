# Art & identity decisions log (Matt's reactions; keep updated every round)

Matt is happy to iterate on style and colour as long as needed until it is right. Every new round must respect everything below and say what it changes.

## Decided
- Identity concept: **The Hollow** (grey drained world; spirit light restores colour and leaves luminous trails; enemies spread corruption; one life-map system). See docs/IDENTITY.md.
- Art family: **stylised 3D-looking** (polished mobile-hit feel, rich lighting, depth). Real 3D vs pre-rendered sprites under test (branch spike-3d).
- Every character and enemy has its own unique, recognisable design. Hero, boss and signature spirits are custom for Hollowlight.
- Everything must fit the look and make mechanical sense (CLAUDE.md standing rule).
- Name: Hollowlight: Spirits of the Dusk. Chapter 1: Cursed Forest.
- Keep from earlier work: modern interface direction is wanted, but the specific UI look is NOT decided.

## Rejected (and why)
- Round 1 UI directions D1-D4 (glass, gothic, neon, ember): better than the original but "still blocky", "too much at once", "no identity of its own".
- Round 2 identity directions E1-E3 (ink, comic cel, papercut): disliked ("I don't like either"); picked "stylised 3D-looking", "too plain/simple", "characters not appealing", "wrong mood for the game".
- Pixel art and the flat vector look: too blocky/plain.
- Original live look (v0.2.0): "boring plain old game".

## Liked
- Wants "Borderlands 4 UI"-like boldness and a clear identity (restraint, one strong idea).
- Liked all three Hollow identity ideas (colour returns, spirit-painted trails, creeping corruption).
- Round 1 mock-ups were "better directions" than the live UI.
