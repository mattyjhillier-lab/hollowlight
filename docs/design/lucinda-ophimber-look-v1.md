## What rounds 1-2 got wrong (both REJECTED by Matt)
- Round 1 (six options) did not match the locked pick. Round 2 ("they look rubbish and not like she's half dragon"; "Ophimber still doesn't look like a dragon") was drawn with rough stand-in helpers: 1-unit lines, tiny horns, a hood that hid the head, freckle dots for scales, a thin tail line, and a blob pet whose 8 px wings blurred away.
- Lesson: draw with the REAL rig craft. Wren/Elias are 122-150 design units (du) tall, 5.6 du tinted ink outlines (fill mixed 70% to #2a0f30), H2 diagonal gradient plus a lower-right rim crescent, 3/4 view, then baked at 1.25 texels/du and shrunk by the camera. The dragon traits must be big SILHOUETTE shapes (horns, fins, spined tail, wings), not surface pattern.
- The old sandbox's "1x" view was about twice the real in-game size. v3 uses the game's own camera rule (zoomFor + DU 0.211): a 1280x720 window shows Wren at 62 px, a 640x360 window at 31 px, a landscape phone (844x390) at 35 px.

## Lucinda: shared traits (all three)
Big ivory horns with ridge rings and rose-gold tips; ember-gold eyes with vertical slit pupils, a warm glow and a thick top lash line; a tiny fang; overlapping scale patches on the cheekbone and the bare forearm; small three-claw hands (one cups her hearth flame); dorsal spines down the back edge of the cloak; a thick scaled tail (cream belly scutes, rose-gold back spines) ending in an outlined rose-gold flame-leaf; scale-hide garment of overlapping plates whose hem ends in a tail-point; clawed boot toes.

- **A Ember Horns, hood down (Recommended).** Jade scale-plate cloak, hood lowered into a spined cowl, long horns swept back with up-hooked tips, chestnut hair, cream ribbed ear-fins, a J-curl tail. Strongest "dragon girl" read: horns, fins, back spines and tail all sit on the silhouette. Risk: jade is the coolest of her colours (contrast passes: 1.86 day / 3.82 night); hair crown can still read a little cap-like at 2x.
- **B Crest Hood, hood up.** Plum scale hood with horn holes and a spiked crest running into the back spines, honey fringe, straight-up crest horns, long low S-tail. Closest to the brief's "hooded cloak" words. Risk: this is the "girl in a patterned hood" look Matt disliked; plum sits 53 from Elias's coat (WARN).
- **C Frill Collar.** Big cream ribbed frill fanned behind her head (frilled-dragon read), sand-gold scale mantle over a teal dress, dark hair, tail raised high behind. Most distinct silhouette. Risk: the frill can read as big fish fins; horns are close in shape to A.

## Ophimber: shared traits (all three)
Coral-ember body, gold-cream belly plates, big glossy eye, short rounded snout with a fang, two curled ridged horns, cheek frill, rose-gold back spines, glowing ember spots, a thick tail with the same flame-leaf tip, three-claw paws, and a PAIR of proper bat wings: an arched leading edge (shoulder, wrist with thumb claw), three finger ribs, scalloped membrane. About 50 du tall (25 px at 1280x720, a third of her height); perches on her back shoulder at her nape, wings half open; in the ability he rises 26 world units with bigger wings and a warm glow.

- **1 Hatchling, four legs (Recommended).** Round loaf body, trots and hops behind her, wings flap as he runs. Most "baby dragon pet". Risk: when perched he is a small clump at true size (head + wing read, not much else).
- **2 Flutter-Wyrm, always flying.** Hovers about 20 du up with a ground shadow, big wings always beating, legs tucked. Wings are the loudest dragon tell at every size; best perched read too (wings spread past her). Risk: always-moving wings add motion noise in a big crowd.
- **3 Plush Drake, sits upright.** Egg body, big striped cream belly, stubby arms, waddles. Cutest and most toy-like. Risk: reads "cute creature" a little before "dragon"; wings are smaller.

## Honest readability at true size (1280x720, magnified view)
- Lucinda A: horns, ear-fin, gold eyes, back spines and the glowing tail tip all read: "horned girl with a dragon tail" in about a second. B: horns and crest read, but the hood dominates. C: frill and raised tail read; horns less so on dark hair. At 640x360 and phone (31-38 px) all three shrink to horns + tail + glow, the same level of detail as Wren and Elias.
- Ophimber 1 and 2: wings plus horns make a clear little dragon when following (25 px). 3 reads as a cute creature with a wing. Perched, all three are smaller and partly overlap her head; 2 reads best.
