## Lucinda (Hearth-Warden)
Distinct from Wren (pale pelt, wolf hood, teal) and Elias (tall rust coat, cream robe, lantern post).

- **A Hearth Mantle (Recommended).** Bell-shaped silhouette: mulberry-rose mantle with a cream scalloped hem and ember-peach lining, chestnut bun with an ember ribbon, a wooden hearth-staff whose iron ring cradles a small flame. Motion: slow hem sway, calm bob, warms her hands at the flame when idle. Why: the bell shape is the only round-bottomed silhouette in the cast (Wren is a hood, Elias a tall rectangle), so she reads at 40 px; the staff flame carries the "warden of a hearth" idea and matches the Wyrm family colours. Risks: staff is the same prop type as Elias's lantern post (mitigated by the ring-and-flame top and the shorter, bell body); rose mantle is the warmest body colour in the cast so keep it out of heart-rose (checked, 49 apart).
- **B Stoker.** Wide-brim hat, cream jerkin, olive sleeves, a small iron stove-pack on her back with a glowing hearth window and a pumping bellows, coral scarf tail. Motion: bouncy, bellows pump with each step. Why: a very readable silhouette (brim plus box plus scarf). Risks: reads as an engineer or adventurer more than a warden, the pack plus pet trail clutters the back-left, and the jerkin sits close to enemy-top grey (48 apart).
- **C Wick Hood.** Tall teardrop hood with a curling flame-wick tip, sea-teal robe with peach flame-stitch trim, big bell sleeves cupping a floating ember. Motion: floats, hood tip lags like a flame, shy. Why: the tallest and most magical, most different from both heroes in shape and colour. Risks: still a hood (Wren has one), the tallest hero grows the hit/scale budget, and teal is the nearest to Wren's tunic blue.

## Ophimber (pet dragon, dragon-kin of the Cinder Wyrm)
Family ties kept in all three: small swept horns, two eye glints, cream horn colour, a tiny ember on the tail tip. All are far smaller and rounder than the Wyrm (7 segments, large, ancestral). Colours are coral/rose/copper with cream bellies (not gold).

- **1 Hearth Hatchling (Recommended).** About 24 units long, 0.4 of her height; a round, four-legged hatchling with stub wings, nubby horns and a flame-tipped tail. Trots at her heel (like Wren's pup but a clearly different shape and colour), hops, wags the tail; in her ability he beats his wings and hovers above her shoulder. Why: friendliest, easiest to read in motion, matches the "follows as the companion model" plan. Risk: the wolf pup is also a heel companion, so Wren (pup) and Lucinda (hatchling) share a role, kept distinct by colour and wings; the tail can read as a fox tail at phone size.
- **2 Shoulder Wyrmling.** About 18 units, noodle-bodied, drapes over her shoulder like a scarf, tail-tip flame. Follows by being carried (zero clutter, cheapest to draw); in her ability he uncoils and rises as a flame column over her head. Closest to the Wyrm's serpent shape. Risk: at 640x360 he is only a few pixels, so face and eye glints are lost, and he adds little personality when walking.
- **3 Kettle Drake.** About 44 units, round and plump with big bat wings and a glowing hearth-window belly; hovers beside her shoulder and bobs, rises higher with wings spread in the ability. Most memorable and the biggest light source. Risks: largest sprite (clutter and occlusion near her), the belly light competes with her staff flame and the evolution gold, and he drifts away from the "small and friendly" brief.
