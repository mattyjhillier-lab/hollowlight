## Ranking (OPINION)
1 Wickwake, 2 Tallowlight, 3 Dawnwick, then Gleamwake, Gloamlight, Kindlewake, Lanternfall, Emberwake (last: exact clashes).
Hollow Knight / Spirit Hunters proximity: none of the eight shares a word with either.

## Top 3
1. **Wickwake (recommended).** Subtitle ideas: "Spirits of the Dusk" (keep, it still fits), or "Light the Grey Back". Fit: wick = the lantern, wake = spirits and colour waking, plus the "wake" of glowing trails. Short, easy to spell and say. Risk: two common words glued together so the web has few clean hits (low clash, but weak search ownership at first); sounds slightly like "Wick". All checks unverified.
2. **Tallowlight.** Subtitle: "A Lantern Against the Grey". Fit: folk, homely candlelight, matches "Folk, not epic" pillar. Risk: "tallow" is an unusual word; some may mispronounce or find it unappealing; "-light" suffix is crowded (Gleamlight, Gloomlight, Hollowlight).
3. **Dawnwick.** Subtitle: "Spirits of the Dusk" (dusk + dawn contrast is nice). Fit: matches the dawn-flood payoff. Risk: near Dawnwalker (big studio release) and Dimwick (candle game); "Dawn-" prefixes are common.
