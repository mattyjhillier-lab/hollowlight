## 1. Husk family rules (shared by every option; how the masks stay clearly NOT Hollow Knight)
Hollow Knight's masks are smooth white rounded shells with horns and big black oval holes, symmetric. Husks are the opposite in every axis:
1. **Grown bark, never smooth white**: pale lichen-bark (#c8c3ad, L* ~78) with carved growth grain following the animal's skull, a chipped/splintered back edge where it grew in, lichen cups, a crack.
2. **Asymmetric**: the two sockets differ in size and height; a chip or crack on one side only.
3. **Per-species shape that follows the animal**: snout (wolf), brow-helmet (crow), plank (crawler), blowpipe (ranged), triangle-holed plate (bulb), deer-skull (hart); never a round head-shell, never horns on the mask (antlers/horns are the animal's own).
4. **Root straps grip the head** (it grew on) and most masks carry one dead twig sprout with a curled grey leaf.
5. **Knot-hole sockets** with a raised bark lip; the eyes are the only face: WL6b ember eye-points (#ff5a3c) float in the void; amber (#ffb84a) only on the miniboss/boss.
Also kept from LOCKED: cold bodies (slate-indigo / grey-blue / lichen grey), red-orange only for eyes + tells, no gore, opaque enemies, dark crow beaks, no mouths/brows (two grain lines that read as a smile/frown were removed during review: ram, rootback, Treant), readable at night via the eyes, ink "socks" (the Hollow got in through the paws).

## 7. Weak spots (honest: what is still below the Wren/Elias bar)
1. **Side views only.** The heroes have a front/3/4/side/back turnaround and 5 expressions; these are one side view plus states. Enemies move in every direction in game (flip only), so this is enough to build, but they look "flatter" than the hero sheets.
2. **The mask reads as a pale SKULL at true size.** The bark grain, chips, lichen and straps (the anti-Hollow-Knight features) only show at 2x and up; at 50 px it is "pale beaked skull + red eyes". Hollow Knight risk is low at true size; a "skull/plague-doctor" read is the more likely comment.
3. **Same eye on every type.** WL6b almond ember eyes with a big halo; at large size they look like "laser eyes" and make every option look related (by design) but less individual. Eye identity comes from count/spacing, not shape.
4. **Puffback Toad's body disappears** under the blister (reads "mushroom on a cushion"); B Puffcap and C Gourd also read as mushroom/bug enemies, which are common.
5. **Box faces**: Slabface (wolf B) and the Briar Ram (hart B) read as a box/visor or a bandaged head; kept as honest alternatives.
6. **Treant hints are still simple** (tube trunk, flat crown): family consistency only; sheet #7 must redo them.
7. **Static poses and approximated night.** Walk/tell/death are drawn poses (no live motion test); night is an SVG colour filter, and the tell decals are darkened with the body here (in the build they draw above the vignette). The WL6d contrast harness must re-measure in the build.
8. **Code-drawn texture limit.** Fur and bark are approximated with tapered locks, grain strokes and noise; it will not match a hand-painted sprite. Best achievable inside the zero-budget/code-only rule; AI concept art would need Matt's explicit approval + provenance checks (not used).
9. **Chest A** may read as a birdhouse at 27 px (its glow still says "reward").
