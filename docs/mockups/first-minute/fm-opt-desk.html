First minute: three questions

1. How we teach

A (Recommended)Lantern whispers: one small plate at a time, triggered by what you do, fades by itself, first time only, never pauses.
BGuided first night: run 1 is scripted for 60 s (few foes, one step at a time, waits for you to move / pick / press).
CPractice dusk: a safe 90 s clearing before the real first run, then the real night starts.

2. First cards and first evolution

A (Recommended)One line above the first offer, NEW tags, rank dots named once; the first gold "stirs" edge gets one line; the first evolution card says what just happened.
BCard anatomy: the first offer slows in with three labelled callouts (spirit, effect, rank) you tap away.
CCodex first: nothing extra in the run; the results screen and codex explain cards and recipes afterwards.

3. Game-feel pass

A (Recommended)Sharp small set (8 items): pops, kick, flourish, hit-stop only on big moments, phone buzz, all bounded and Calm-aware.
BBig juice pass: squash/stretch on every hit, knockback, slow-mo on level-up, flashes and trails everywhere.
CTune only: no new effects; measure and re-tune what is built (shake, stop, numbers, sounds).
