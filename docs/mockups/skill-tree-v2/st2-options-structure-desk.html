Decision 1 of 3 - overall structure
How the whole tree is laid out. Same nodes, budget and gates in all three.
A. Lantern and five constellationsRECOMMENDED
Hub lantern, two global rings, five weapon emblems around them, keystones on the outer dusk ring.
+ Matches the locked v2 row (clusters + global rings) and Matt's Spirit Hunters web; every weapon is its own place; grows outward forever (new sky band per chapter).
− Widest canvas: needs zoom levels (far / mid / near) and a Next-lights board so new players are not lost.
B. Lantern tree (vertical)
One trunk rising from the heroes' roots to dawn; each weapon is a branch, evolutions blossom at the tips.
+ Very easy on a phone (scroll up only); clear order; strong "tree" read.
− Not a pannable web (conflicts with the locked meta-progression principle); emblems squashed into branches; long scroll at 500 nodes.
C. The Hollow map
The tree is a painted map of the Hollow: each weapon is a landmark region you restore; nodes are lanterns along paths.
+ Strongest identity link (colour returns to the map as you buy).
− Heaviest art; emblem constellations get lost in terrain; confusable with the front-door map select; 2-3x the build cost.
