Decision 2 of 3 - node and emblem look
How a constellation is drawn. Shown on the wolf; all five emblems use the same language.
A. Lantern stars on glowing threadRECOMMENDED
Brass-rimmed glass beads joined by a glowing thread that traces the emblem; a soft emblem watermark fills with role colour as you buy.
+ Reuses the locked kit (brass, glass, thread, wick glow) and the in-game trail glow; reads at phone size; cheap to draw (circles + lines).
− The emblem is mostly implied by the thread, so it needs the watermark to read at far zoom.
B. Stained-glass emblem
The emblem is a leadlight window; each lantern is a pane that fills with colour when bought.
+ Huge wow; colour-returns reads instantly.
− Conflicts with a lock: the leadlight border is reserved for evolution cards and the dawn screen (UI round 2); irregular panes are poor tap targets; far harder to author for every new weapon.
C. Embroidered sampler
The tree is cloth: the emblem is a running stitch, lanterns are French knots, owned stitches glow gold-cream.
+ Warm, handmade, leans on the thread signature.
− Low contrast on phones; cloth is a new material not in the kit; gold stitches fight the evolution-only gold rule.
