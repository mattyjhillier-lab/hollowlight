Difficulty tiers: the three first decisions

1. How tiers work
RECOMMENDEDA. Three nights per map
Twilight / Deep Dusk / Long Night (names provisional).
Each = a data block: tougher foes + ONE readable new rule (Hollow-marked foes; then less heart luck + a 4th mark).
Easy to read, phone friendly, bot-measurable.
B. The Gloom dial (Hades-style)
Players switch on Omens (8-10 toggles, each worth points).
Deep Dusk = 5+ points, Long Night = 10+.
Experts love it; much more UI and a huge balance matrix.
C. Ascending nights 1-10
Slay the Spire ladder per map: each night adds one rule.
Win night N to open N+1. Deep = 1-5, Long = 6-10.
Clear goal, but ten steps to balance and a long climb.
2. New resource
RECOMMENDEDA. One: Dawnglass
Morning light caught in glass; drops ONLY on Deep Dusk / Long Night (boss, dawn, first dawns, a few marked foes).
Buys the Daybreak lanterns (Fourth Card, Fifth Hook, Long Night gate, keystones) + cosmetic tints.
Never on Twilight, no conversion, capped per run.
dawnglass
B. Two: Wax + Dawnglass
Candle wax on Deep Dusk, dawnglass on Long Night, each with its own ring.
More to collect, more identity.
Two new purses on screen; harder to balance; risk of a dead currency.
wax
C. None: bigger gold and shards
Harder tiers pay x1.5 / x2 gold and more shards; tier clears are only conditions.
Simplest, no new purse.
Weakest pull to play harder: gold already buys everything.
x1.5+2
3. Where tier power works
RECOMMENDEDA. Only on the tiers that pay it
Fourth Card, Fifth Hook and the Long Night keystones work on Deep Dusk / Long Night only.
Twilight balance never moves (the locked ceiling holds); QoL and info nodes work everywhere.
Odd at first: "why only 3 cards on Twilight?"
B. Everywhere, inside one ceiling
Everything works on every tier; to fit, knots and tags are trimmed harder and the 4th card / 5th slot become exclusive keystones (pick one).
Simple rule; weaker nodes.
C. Everywhere, ceiling raised
Everything works everywhere and the ceiling rises +2.5 by rule.
Feels best to own; Twilight gets easier for veterans.
Needs a reopen of "the ceiling still holds".
