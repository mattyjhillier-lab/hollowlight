## 1. How the genre makes an evolution feel WANTED

| # | Finding | Games | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The evolution is an EVENT, not just another card: VS gives it from a boss chest (chest-opening fanfare, coins, the evolved weapon is the first reward); HoloCure collabs need a Golden Anvil drop and an anvil menu where eligible weapons shine | VS, HoloCure | COMMUNITY (wikis) | vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution ; holocure.wiki.gg/wiki/Collabs |
| 2 | Clear prerequisites the player can SEE progressing: max weapon + its passive (VS); two weapons at level 7+ start anvil drops, eligible weapons glow (HoloCure); a collab list in the level-up menu since v0.6 | VS, HoloCure | COMMUNITY | same |
| 3 | Recipes are partly hidden but become known: VS players learn them from the in-game collection / community lists; the spin-off Vampire Crawlers has a Grimoire recipe book that shows discovered recipes in-run | VS family | COMMUNITY (guides) / RECALLED for VS collection | dotesports, keengamer (Vampire Crawlers Grimoire) |
| 4 | The evolved weapon replaces the base (frees nothing, but looks different: new sprite and usually extra effects, wider area, sometimes a new behaviour such as lifesteal) | VS | COMMUNITY | vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution |
| 5 | Collabs are "treated as max level" and lock their components out of the level-up pool: the pick is final and simplifies later offers | HoloCure | COMMUNITY | holocure.wiki.gg/wiki/Collabs |
| 6 | Spirit Hunters gives each skill two "mods" that unlock after a skill level; skills max at level 25; reviewers complained +1% upgrades felt unimpactful | Spirit Hunters | COMMUNITY (review, guide) | seasonedgaming.com review ; gamepretty.com basic guide |
| 7 | Best-loved evolutions CHANGE BEHAVIOUR, not just numbers; stat-bump evolutions are remembered as boring | VS, HoloCure | RECALLED + OPINION (also our file 02/23) | - |
| 8 | Power spike timing: first evolution ~1/3 into the run (VS ~10 of 30 min) | VS | COMMUNITY (file 02) | primagames |

What creates desire (OPINION, distilled): (a) anticipation you can watch fill (rank pips + "Something stirs"), (b) a moment (time-slow, sound, flash), (c) the new verb is shown at once (first pounce/strike happens right after the pick), (d) a new silhouette that you can point at on screen, (e) a clear "this is better" signal at the card (one honest number), (f) no hidden cost that makes you regret it (losing coverage, losing boss damage).

## 2. Readable stat upgrades

| # | Finding | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brotato writes burn as "5x8 +(100% Elemental Damage)": ticks x damage, so the player can do the maths; Eyes Surgery says ticks get faster (0.8 s/0.6 s) and the wiki spells out "+25% / +67% burn DPS, same total" | COMMUNITY (wiki quoting item text) | brotato.wiki.spellsandguns.com/Burning |
| 2 | Soulstone tooltips like "+180 Burn damage" really mean "applies 2 stacks"; players call the tooltips misleading and complain a boss shows one Bleed icon with thousands of stacks and no source split | COMMUNITY (fan wiki + Steam) | soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Powers ; Steam threads 2022-2024 |
| 3 | Path of Exile's ignite rework was explained as fixing "large amounts of negative player feedback" because ignite ignored "increased fire damage" and was "tricky for new players to understand" | COMMUNITY (preview article quoting devs) | gamebanshee.com/k8hxc (via search) |
| 4 | Spirit Hunters: tiny +1% upgrades read as unimpactful | COMMUNITY | seasonedgaming review |

Rule we take (OPINION): every stat card says what CHANGES in plain words and one number the player can feel ("Bite 10 -> 13 damage", "Bleed lasts 4 s and deals 32% of the bite each second"). A stat that does nothing visible in normal play (our wolf speed, bolt speed, chain jump range, bleed time; see review doc) is a trap card, worse than no card.

## 3. Status effects: how games keep DoT and control balanced and readable

| # | Finding | Games | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Burn does NOT stack: a new burn on a burning target keeps the higher damage AND the higher duration (a weak burn can extend a strong one) | Brotato | COMMUNITY (wiki) | brotato.wiki.spellsandguns.com/Burning |
| 2 | Burn cannot crit or life-steal; spreads only via specific items with a SHARED spread counter (3 snakes = 3 spreads per burn) | Brotato | COMMUNITY | same |
| 3 | Ignite: only the highest-DPS instance deals damage (even across players); poison is the deliberate exception that stacks independently; PoE2 bleed/ignite one instance by default | Path of Exile 1/2 | COMMUNITY (poewiki) | poewiki.net/wiki/Ailment ; poe2wiki.net/wiki/Ignite |
| 4 | Hades curses each belong to ONE god (Doom=Ares, Chill=Demeter, Hangover=Dionysus, Weak=Aphrodite, Jolted=Zeus, Marked=Artemis, Exposed=Athena); duo boons are the designed cross-status interactions | Hades | COMMUNITY (Fextralife) | hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Status+Effects |
| 5 | Hangover (Hades II) stacks to 5, stacks fall off one at a time; a boon raises the cap (to 8 on Weak foes in Hades I): stack caps are a tuning lever that the tree/boons raise | Hades I/II | COMMUNITY (guides, mixed versions) | mobalytics.gg Hades 2 curses ; twinfinite Dionysus guide |
| 6 | Weak = foes deal >=30% less damage, 1 s, reapplied freely: control-ish statuses are short and refresh-only | Hades II | COMMUNITY | rogueranker.com Hades 2 status effects |
| 7 | Soulstone: statuses come from powers; "spread to all attacks" upgrades only appear once you own an ability that applies that status (ownership gating) | Soulstone | COMMUNITY (Steam 2024) | Steam threads (app 2066020) |
| 8 | Halls of Torment: burn builds race to a stack cap (players assume 20); quest text counts stacks | Halls of Torment | COMMUNITY (Steam, unconfirmed) | steamcommunity.com/app/2218750 discussion |
| 9 | Dead Cells / Undecember: DoTs stack with each other and themselves (high power, high complexity) | other ARPG/action | COMMUNITY | deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Oil |

Patterns (OPINION): (1) **one owner per status** (Hades) keeps identity and prevents everything stacking with everything; (2) **strongest-wins + refresh** (Brotato burn, PoE ignite/bleed) is the readable default; stacking is the rare, named exception; (3) **stack caps and spread counters** are the safety valves; (4) **control is short and refresh-only**; bosses resist control; (5) players hate unattributed numbers and misleading tooltips (Soulstone), so DoT ticks need their own colour and a clear source.

## 4. Status nodes in meta trees

| Finding | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hades: Mirror/boons raise status caps (Hangover stack cap) rather than raw damage; duo boons add one interaction | COMMUNITY | as 3.5 |
| Soulstone: status upgrades only appear once you own a source (no dead nodes) | COMMUNITY | as 3.7 |
| Our own rule (progression-gates-v1): stat nodes 1-3%, soft cap per category, combat PE ceiling 15.5 | PROJECT | docs/design/progression-gates-v1.md 3.1 |

Implication (OPINION): status tree nodes should be per STATUS (Bleed, Burn, Mark), each in one shared capped "status potency" category, gated by playing that status's owner (e.g. "kill 300 bleeding enemies"), and should prefer duration-cap/stack-cap/visual verbs over raw %.
