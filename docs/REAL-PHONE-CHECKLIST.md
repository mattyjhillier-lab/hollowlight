# Real-phone checklist: the Lantern Web (tree home) and what-moved screens

For Matt, on a real phone, test link = the dev build (dev.hollowlight-12n.pages.dev, unlisted). Ten minutes, one pass. Tick, write what felt wrong in one line.
What the computer already checked (headless, so not a substitute): every button in the tree and the tour is at least 44 px, Tab / keyboard path, Calm loop stops, 0 console errors, tree draw 1.3 ms desktop (9 ms with a 4x slowed CPU).

## 0. Before you start
- [ ] Note the phone model, browser, battery %, and whether Battery Saver is on.
- [ ] Use a lantern with some progress (not brand new) AND, separately, a brand new lantern (Settings > Light a new lantern).

## 1. Brand new lantern (the first minute)
- [ ] Title > Play: you pick a hero, then a spirit (nothing preselected), then land on the tree with your spirit lit and a small tour card.
- [ ] The tour card: 4 steps, Next and Skip tour are easy to hit with a thumb, the card does not hide what it points at.
- [ ] Back out of the tour, tap the gold Play pill at the top left of the tree, pick the hero and spirit again, walk: does the extra step feel like too much? (Note it: the recommended trim is skipping the hero step when only one hero is awake.)

## 2. Touch targets and layout (tree)
- [ ] Every button is easy to hit first time: the right rail (+ - sun path ?), the can-light badge, Skill tabs, bottom sheet buttons (Light it, Pin goal, the arrow).
- [ ] Tap a lantern: the bottom sheet opens small (name, state, price); the arrow expands it to the condition tile (picture, "37 of 1,000 grave crows", In the Almanac).
- [ ] Nothing important sits under the notch, the home bar or the rounded corners (portrait AND landscape).
- [ ] Landscape: no clipped text; the sheet is usable.

## 3. Pinch zoom, pan, performance
- [ ] Two-finger pinch zooms smoothly; dragging pans; it never feels stuck or jumpy. Zoom all the way out (rings and names stay readable?) and all the way in.
- [ ] Double-tap on empty sky zooms in; tapping a crowded spot zooms in on the cluster instead of guessing.
- [ ] Open the tree, drag around for 30 seconds on the busiest part (the hub with many lit lanterns): note any stutter. `?perf=1` on the link shows frame ms and the worst frame.
- [ ] Rotate the phone while the tree is open: the view re-fits, nothing breaks.

## 4. Heat and battery (the tree is a canvas that redraws while things shimmer)
- [ ] Leave the tree open and idle for 5 minutes with the screen on: the phone should not get noticeably warm. The ring shimmer is the only continuous motion (and it stops with Calm motion).
- [ ] Note battery % at the start and after 10 minutes of the tree + a run.
- [ ] Battery Saver ON: everything still works (the browser may slow animation: note it).
- [ ] Settings > Calm motion ON: no shimmer, no pulses, no moving dashes, the camera jumps instead of flying, the tour card just appears. (Also try the phone's own Reduce Motion setting.)

## 5. After a run (what moved)
- [ ] Die once, win once. The results card shows a "Next lights" block: chips of what you gained, lanterns that became affordable, bars that grew, the closest unlocks. It fits on one screen (portrait). In landscape it may drop: tell me if that is annoying.
- [ ] Tap Tree: the camera flies to what moved and rings it for a few seconds. Calm: it jumps and the ring is steady.
- [ ] Pause during a run: the same list (read only, nothing to tap) shows the closest unlocks.

## 6. Colour and reading
- [ ] Threads: lit (bright, glowing), can light (medium), locked (faint). Can you tell the three apart outside in daylight? Low brightness?
- [ ] Phone's colour-blind or greyscale mode (Accessibility): the three thread states and the chips still read.
- [ ] Text size: set the phone to a large text size; the tour card, the sheet and the results list still fit.

## 7. Screen reader (if you use one)
- [ ] TalkBack / VoiceOver: opening the tree reads "N of M lanterns lit, K can light now"; selecting a lantern reads its name, state and effect; buying says it is lit.

## 8. Report back
Phone + browser, anything that stuttered, anything too small, anything that got hot, anything that made you stop. One line each is plenty.
